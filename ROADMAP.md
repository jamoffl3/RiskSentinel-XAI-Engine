# RiskSentinel AI Roadmap

## Vision

Build an Explainable Governance Intelligence Platform capable of identifying organizational, operational, compliance, and governance risks through a combination of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Explainable AI, and predictive analytics.

The long-term goal is to create a system that not only detects risks but also explains why those risks exist and recommends actions to mitigate them.
